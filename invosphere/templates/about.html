Our Story

TastyPay was born out of a simple idea — to make restaurant billing faster, smarter, and more enjoyable for both customers and owners.

Built under the Invosphere project, TastyPay aims to transform how billing and sales data are managed — bringing innovation, automation, and insights to the food industry.
